### Ledger

**AI reference:** The Ledger records anything from the turn that materially changes what should appear on the character sheet.

If the event would require the character sheet to be updated, record that change here.

This includes, but is not limited to:

* changes to a Capability, Sub-skill, Specialty, Development, or other recorded ability;
* new training, experience, education, or established competence;
* wounds, illness, disability, recovery, treatment, fatigue, or another lasting physical condition;
* changes to appearance or physical features;
* aging when relevant to the character sheet;
* changes in title, office, rank, legal status, social standing, allegiance, or reputation;
* new or lost relationships, kinship, marriage, wards, obligations, oaths, debts, or claims;
* equipment, clothing, weapons, armor, property, animals, retainers, or possessions being gained, lost, damaged, replaced, or materially altered;
* changes to money, income, property, or other recorded resources when those are part of the character sheet;
* important knowledge, languages, literacy, or education becoming established;
* any other durable fact that changes the character as recorded.

Record only the material change.

Do not repeat information merely because it remains true.

Every Ledger change should also be applied to the character sheet or relevant persistent character record.

If nothing on the character sheet materially changes during the turn, omit the Ledger entirely.
