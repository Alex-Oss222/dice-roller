# Shared book references

All stories use the same reference policy and available source catalog. This file contains no story's outcomes or alternate history. It is a reference index, not a copy of the novels. No complete book texts have been supplied or bundled with this build.

The published main-series works that a story may explicitly permit are:

- A Game of Thrones
- A Clash of Kings
- A Storm of Swords
- A Feast for Crows
- A Dance with Dragons

Published companion works can be selected explicitly when relevant, including The World of Ice & Fire, Fire & Blood, and A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms. This list is a selectable catalog, not blanket permission to use every title or to reveal all its events. Record the specific permitted works and the spoiler cutoff in each story's setup. Distinguish book continuity from television continuity.

Use only passages actually available and checked. Record a work and chapter/section locator when genuinely consulted. A secondary website that cites a book remains a secondary source unless the underlying text was checked. Do not invent quotations, page numbers, access to unavailable texts, or a complete book corpus. Follow [research guidance](../docs/research.md) for evidence strength and safe sourcing.

The catalog and research method are shared. Their application is local: each story decides its era, location, permitted sources, cutoff, and causal divergences. Story 1's ruler, death, alliance, discovered secret, interpretation, or invented custom never updates this index or becomes a fact of Story 2. Record those in the selected story's events and notes with their scope.

A genuine correction to this catalog is shared maintenance. It must be supported independently of a story's desired outcome and reviewed for existing story baselines. Never edit a book source or mark a campaign invention as canon to accommodate a protagonist.
