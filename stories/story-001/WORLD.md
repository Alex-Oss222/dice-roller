# Story 1 · World

This campaign is independent of Story 2. These are its opening facts; later lasting consequences belong here.

## Opening position

Turn 0, 18:00 on the 1st day of the first moon, 283 AC. This exact calendar date is the existing campaign's convention. The opening is the evening before the expected battle at the Trident, not after a predetermined victory.

Ned is in his command tent in the northern section of the rebel encampment beside the river. The scene has stopped before he gives orders about an overdue patrol.

## Political and social position

- Aerys II remains king. His government regards Ned and the other rebel leaders as traitors. Aerys had Rickard and Brandon Stark killed and demanded Ned's and Robert's deaths.
- The rebel coalition joins the forces of Stark, Baratheon, Arryn, and Tully. Robert is Ned's ally and foster brother, not yet his king. Allied leaders retain authority over their own men.
- Ned inherited Winterfell and its obligations unexpectedly. Northern lords answered his summons, but retain households, interests, and responsibilities of their own.
- Ned recently married Catelyn Tully at Riverrun after the Battle of the Bells. The marriage binds the houses politically; personal familiarity between them is still limited.
- Lyanna remains missing. Ned knows the account that Rhaegar took her, not her whereabouts or the truth of her circumstances.

## Army and ground

- Ned commands the northern contingent. Instructions to allied troops require their commanders' agreement.
- Rhaegar commands the royal army nearby. A major battle is expected the next day. Its course and outcome have not occurred in this campaign.
- The Trident and its approaches constrain movement of men, horses, and baggage. Scouts' observations do not provide a complete view of the opposite bank or every crossing.
- The camp is preparing weapons, armor, mounts, sentries, and orders. Exact troop strengths, enemy dispositions, and usable routes have not yet been verified in this record.

## Immediate unresolved matter

Two northern scouts sent to examine an approach to the river have missed their agreed return. Their captain reports that they were not ordered to cross. Their fate is unknown; being overdue does not establish capture, desertion, or enemy action.

Two other riders are waiting for instructions at the picket line. One of their mounts is lame and needs replacing before it can be used for the proposed search. No relief party or new order has been authorized by Ned. The captain wants to recover the scouts without losing more men before the battle.

## Economic opening state

This is the campaign's qualitative 283 AC snapshot under [the shared economic model](../../references/ECONOMY.md).

Winterfell and House Stark's lands, household, and associated property remain under Ned's lordship. They are a source of obligations and support, not a purse entirely available in his tent. His immediate campaigning resources are with the northern host; his assigned riding horse is at its picket line.

Mobilized men and horses need provisions, fodder, transport, and care while away from their holdings. The coalition's presence beside the river makes access to routes and supplies consequential. Allied resources are not automatically Ned's to command, and war can interrupt local movement and purchasing.
