# Prepared opening, not a played turn

This is staged prose for the later start instruction. No setup event or player action has been accepted. The exact text below is stored in `setup.json` as `opening_narrative`.

Eddard Stark sits in his command tent in the northern section of the rebel encampment beside the Trident. The evening meal is over. His steel longsword lies across his knees. He works the edge with a whetstone, drawing it along the blade and returning it for another pass.

His field armor is nearby: fitted breastplate and backplate, articulated limb defenses, mail for the gaps, and a padded arming garment. The helmet and gauntlets lie with the harness. His dagger and heater shield are within reach. He wears ordinary campaign clothes. The armor is still off.

His travel gear is in the tent. The assigned riding horse and its tack are at the northern picket line. Outside, men check armor, tend weapons, and feed and water the horses. Captains pass orders and assign sentries as preparations continue through the camp.

The royal army is nearby under Prince Rhaegar Targaryen. A major engagement is expected the next day. Robert Baratheon, Jon Arryn, Hoster Tully, and Ned lead the rebel coalition; the northern forces are under Ned's command.
