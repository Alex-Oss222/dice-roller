# Story 1: pre-Trident source check

Accessed: **22 September 2026**. Scope: the supplied Eddard Stark character sheet, immediately before the expected Battle of the Trident. This is story-local research preparation, not an accepted setup event or new character knowledge. Linked pages cover more history than this note; only the pre-start claims below are adopted for consideration.

## Birth year and approximate age

- **Claim:** The seed's 263 AC birth year and approximately twenty years of age in 283 AC are supportable.
- **Source:** [A Wiki of Ice and Fire, Eddard Stark birth calculation](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Years_after_Aegon%27s_Conquest/Calculations_Ages_%28Continued%29#Eddard_Stark).
- **Support actually checked:** The wiki derives 263 AC by combining its cited age evidence with Eddard being born a year after Brandon, whose birth it dates to 262 AC. It explicitly presents 263 AC as its resulting chronology.
- **Source type and confidence:** Secondary reference with cited underlying works; moderate confidence from this check. The underlying book/app passages were not independently consulted here.
- **Limit:** Approximately twenty is an inference from the supplied year and that chronology. No birthday or precise date for the opening evening was verified. Preserve the approximation.

## Fosterage, Robert, and Jon Arryn

- **Claim:** Fosterage with Jon Arryn at the Eyrie from eight, alongside Robert, is supported by a cited secondary account.
- **Source:** [A Wiki of Ice and Fire, Eddard Stark: Youth](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Eddard_Stark#Youth).
- **Support actually checked:** The entry states the starting age, identifies Robert as a fellow ward, and describes Jon as a second father. It cites *A Game of Thrones*, Catelyn I and Catelyn II, for this background.
- **Source type and confidence:** Secondary, moderate confidence from this check. Those chapters were not independently read here. This verifies the source's support, not an exhaustive primary-text audit.

## Visits home and residence after sixteen

- **Claim:** The seed's statement that Ned divided time between Winterfell and the Vale after sixteen has specific author support.
- **Source:** [So Spake Martin, Some Questions, 16 March 2000](https://www.westeros.org/Citadel/SSM/Entry/Some_Questions).
- **Support actually checked:** The archived response says Ned visited home during fosterage, increasingly often and for longer periods as he grew older. From sixteen, he was free to travel and spent time both at home and in the Vale. It also describes travel with Jon Arryn during his earlier training years.
- **Source type and confidence:** Author correspondence reproduced in a third-party archive; strong support for the limited claim. The original correspondence was not separately authenticated.
- **Limit:** This supplies no complete itinerary, fixed residence schedule, or quantified skill-training history. Retain the background sentence without converting it into invented years of specialist practice.

## The Bells before the marriage

- **Claim:** Ned's participation at the Battle of the Bells precedes his marriage to Catelyn at Riverrun.
- **Source:** [A Wiki of Ice and Fire, Eddard Stark: Robert's Rebellion](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Eddard_Stark#Robert.27s_Rebellion).
- **Support actually checked:** The entry places Ned and Hoster Tully at the Bells, followed by Ned's journey to Riverrun for the marriage. It cites *A Storm of Swords*, Arya V, for the battle account and *A Game of Thrones*, Catelyn II, for the marriage.
- **Source type and confidence:** Secondary, moderate confidence from this check. The cited chapters were not independently consulted.
- **Limit:** This supports the sequence; it does not establish an exact interval between the wedding and this story's opening evening. Keep “recently married” as a broad starting description rather than assigning a day count.

## Ice and the sword in the tent

- **Claim:** Treating Ice primarily as a ceremonial/execution weapon has author-comment support; its presence in this Trident camp remains unverified.
- **Source:** [So Spake Martin, Stockholm and Archipelacon Report, 28 June 2015](https://www.westeros.org/Citadel/SSM/Entry/12432), the reported question about Ned using Ice in battle.
- **Support actually checked:** Elio's report attributes to Martin a description of Ice as an unusually large, cumbersome greatsword primarily suited to executions. Martin's reported wording about combat practicality is qualified. The stronger conclusion that Ned never fought with it is the reporter's interpretation.
- **Source type and confidence:** Eyewitness report of author remarks, not a verbatim independently checked recording; moderate confidence for the limited characterization.
- **Limit:** The report does not locate Ice on the eve of the Trident. Do not identify the sword being sharpened as Ice, grant its immediate availability, or turn a qualified comment into a universal mechanical prohibition. The sheet's distinction between inherited ownership and unestablished field location is appropriate.

## Boundaries of this check

The precise calendar date, season, camp layout, current equipment locations, immediate troop disposition, and personal cash were not independently verified. The opening tent scene remains the supplied campaign premise. Height, capability ratings, aptitude, Development, and Condition ratings remain explicit campaign judgments; these sources do not determine their numerical values. No later outcome is included or made inevitable.
