## Background

Ned grew up between Winterfell and the Eyrie. Jon Arryn took him into his household at eight, and Robert Baratheon became his closest friend. Ned performed the work of a page and squire, accompanied Jon on journeys, and continued to spend time in the North. After sixteen, his northern stays became longer.

The deaths of Rickard and Brandon Stark made Ned Lord of Winterfell before he expected to inherit. He called the northern banners and marched south. By the evening before the battle at the Trident, he has helped relieve Robert at the Bells, joined the wider rebel coalition, and married Catelyn Tully at Riverrun.

His upbringing in two great households gave him sustained exposure to arms, noble obligations, petitions, customary disputes, household administration, history, heraldry, and the practical expectations placed on a highborn heir or lord. Campaigning has since added real experience of exhaustion, logistics, formations, battlefield confusion, and command.

## Established experience

These are the abilities and limits established by Ned’s upbringing and experience. Judge an attempt from that evidence and the actual circumstances; expertise in one activity does not supply expertise in another.

### Arms and command

Ned gave particular attention to sword practice throughout his upbringing. Repeated work with household men-at-arms made his footwork, distance, and recovery dependable under pressure. Training in armor taught him its restrictions before campaigning exposed him to exhaustion and battlefield disorder.

Raising and moving his own host, then joining allied forces at the Bells, gave him real command experience. He can recognize a formation losing order and issue useful instructions while frightened men are still trying to understand what has happened. His relationship with Robert and Jon helps coordination, but gives him neither authority over their men nor knowledge of orders they have not shared.

His strongest weapon is the longsword. He has practical training in formation fighting, mounted combat, and two-handed sword use, without exceptional specialization in those forms. His archery experience is limited. None of this makes unfamiliar weapons, surprise, several opponents, injury, or exhaustion harmless.

### Law, lordship, and negotiation

Service in Winterfell and the Eyrie gave Ned sustained education in obligations, petitions, inheritance, allegiance, precedent, and customary disputes. Hearing both sides and working through competing claims became a particular strength. He can identify points of agreement, explain what a settlement requires, and distinguish an insult from a claim that needs answering.

His manner is generally quiet and direct. He is less accomplished at public oratory, detecting a practiced lie, or bargaining over a merchant's margins. A sound settlement still requires people willing or compelled to keep it.

### Political judgment and discretion

Ned can reason about competing interests, concealed motives, political leverage, secrecy, and what different people stand to gain or lose. He can investigate, compare reports, restrict sensitive information, and decide who needs to know what through ordinary household and military channels.

He usually gives duties, promises, custom, and declared intentions real weight. That may delay his recognition of bad faith; it does not make him incapable of understanding intrigue once he has reason to suspect it. Detecting a practiced lie is weaker than his legal judgment. He has little personal experience of professional espionage, covert networks, or countersurveillance.

### Supply and administration

Campaigning has made provisions, baggage, horses, delays, and allocation immediate responsibilities. Ned understands those dependencies and can organize work around them. His experience with a great household's accounts is narrower, and he has had little time to govern Winterfell personally as its lord.

He can question a tally, understand ordinary estate or campaign figures, and plan an allocation. He does not possess a farmer's detailed agricultural knowledge or an experienced steward's familiarity with every estate problem.

He routinely maintains his own sword and riding gear. Forging a blade, making armor, and repairing badly damaged armor require trades he has not learned.

### Travel and fieldcraft

Repeated northern travel gave Ned practical experience with woodland travel, exposure, trails, shelter, and rough ground. He became particularly good at preserving strength during difficult travel and noticing signs of recent passage. The march south added experience reading terrain for men, horses, and baggage.

These strengths are uneven. He is an experienced rider and a serviceable hunter and mountain traveller, but knowledge of a region's shape and principal roads does not guarantee precise navigation in unfamiliar country. He has less experience with covert scouting than with travelling openly with companions or troops.

### Faith, education, and languages

Ned has a good working knowledge of history, heraldry, and noble genealogy from sustained highborn education. He has no specialist medical training or universal scholarly expertise.

The old gods belong to Ned's family life and northern upbringing. His knowledge is strongest in customary observance, oaths, sacred places, and inherited stories. It gives him no priestly office and no special knowledge of forgotten magic.

His education included sustained tuition in the Old Tongue, including conversation and written practice. He reads, writes, and speaks it fluently. He is fluent in spoken High Valyrian and competent, though less assured, with difficult written passages. Knowing a language does not give him facts concealed in an unfamiliar text.

## Physical baseline

Approximately 6'3”, tall and fit from sustained training and campaigning. He has a trained fighting man's strength and practiced movement in the yard and saddle, without extraordinary strength or quickness. Long journeys, marches, and fighting in armor have built his endurance.

His practical awareness is strongest around terrain, command, and immediate physical circumstances; it is not uncanny. He is an educated lord capable of independent judgment, not an exceptional scholar. After his father and brother were killed, he assumed the lordship, raised his banners, and continued the campaign.
