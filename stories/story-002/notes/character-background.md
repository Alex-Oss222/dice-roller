# Jon Snow — background and the reasoning behind the ratings

The sheet's Experience section is the description; this note says why the numbers are what they are, and which Jon they describe.

## Book Jon, not show Jon

The sheet follows the novels. The television version made Jon a taciturn master swordsman and stripped the wit, the pride and the watchfulness; readers' comparisons (Collider, the r/asoiaf thread, the ladystarks essay, Quora) agree on the same points, and the novel's own text supports them: at the start of *A Game of Thrones* Jon's sword is a trained boy's, his edge over untrained recruits comes from Ser Rodrik's teaching (Donal Noye tells him so, Jon III), his strength is his mind and his eyes (Benjen says the Watch could use a man as observant as he is, Jon I), and he has a dry, sardonic humour he mostly keeps to himself. He is also proud, quick-tempered when his bastardy is touched, and does not yet know how much his upbringing gave him. Those are the traits the Disposition section records.

## Method

Inference from the record as it stands on the morning the story opens, scaled to a fourteen-year-old. Every rating is one of these facts:

- Longsword 4 and the Martial domain at 3. Daily drill under a master-at-arms; an even match with Robb (Bran I: "graceful and quick where his half brother was strong and fast"); never in real danger. No strong aptitude: the books say the advantage is training, not talent. The show's swordsman is not on this sheet.
- History 4 with strong aptitude, Geography 3, Learning 4. The novels show him well read beyond his lessons: the Kings of Winter, the First Men, the Free Cities, the Young Dragon. Aptitude here is the one forward-looking judgment on the sheet, and it is where the books put his talent.
- Detecting Lies 3, Concealment 3, Intrigue 2. Benjen's "observant"; a bastard's habit of watching and keeping his face still. Nothing trained.
- Fieldcraft 3. A boy who rides and hunts near Winterfell, not a ranger. Navigation and Survival at 2 because he has never been beyond a known road or spent a cold night alone.
- Diplomacy 3, Craft 2, no numeric Stewardship. The conduct of a great house learned from inside it; a boy who keeps his own gear; nothing of running a household.

Nothing on the sheet is a later-book fact: no direwolf, no raven, no royal party, no oath, no mother.

## Languages: the player's premise

The novels establish only the Common Tongue for Jon. The player has chosen, as a standing preference across their stories, that Jon speaks, reads and writes the Old Tongue, High Valyrian and Low Valyrian fluently. The sheet records that as a story premise with a plausible route (the maester's books, tuition, Free Cities traders in the North) and the setup assumptions say it is not canon. Low Valyrian is a family of regional dialects rather than one written language; the sheet treats it as the trade tongue of the Free Cities in its Braavosi and Pentoshi forms.
