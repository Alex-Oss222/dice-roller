# Story 2 · World

This campaign is independent of Story 1. Its past follows its own established opening, not any later choices made in the 283 AC campaign.

## Opening position

Turn 0, about 08:00 on a morning in the ninth year of summer, 298 AC. No more exact calendar date has been established.

Jon is in the yard of a holdfast in the hills north of Winterfell. Eddard has brought Robb, Jon, Theon, Bran, and household guards to witness the execution of a Night's Watch deserter. The sentence has not yet been carried out. Bran has asked Jon whether leaving was the man's only offense; Jon has not answered.

## Authority and household

- Robert Baratheon is king. Eddard is Lord of Winterfell and Warden of the North, with responsibility for justice in his lands.
- Desertion from the Night's Watch is treated as a capital offense here. Eddard has come to carry out the sentence himself.
- Robb is Winterfell's heir. Jon is protected and maintained as Ned's acknowledged bastard son, without lands, an inheritance, or independent command.
- Catelyn has not accepted Jon's place in the household. Jon's close ties with Robb, Arya, and Bran do not remove that dependence on his father.
- Theon is Eddard's ward and hostage following the Greyjoy rebellion. He accompanies the household but is not Jon's friend.
- Benjen serves as First Ranger at the Wall. Jon has considered joining him, but has made no commitment.
- Winterfell's latest information still names Jon Arryn as Hand. Jon has received no newer news; that information does not establish what has happened elsewhere since it was sent.

## Holdfast and prisoner

The holdfast is within reach of Winterfell by the household's morning ride. No measured distance or complete route has been established. The party has horses and household guards; Jon carries riding clothes and a belt-knife, not his practice arms or armor.

The prisoner is held by the guards and struggles to stand without support. He has admitted leaving without orders. He says there were three of them and that he left the others; their fate and the circumstances of his flight have not been established by evidence available to Jon. Nothing in the opening supplies Jon with proof of supernatural events.

Jory is managing the guards and preparations. Eddard is beside the block with Ice. The scene is paused before the execution, so Jon can still speak or act. No intervention, reply to Bran, or other player decision has been supplied.
