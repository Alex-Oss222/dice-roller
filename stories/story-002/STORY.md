## Turn 0 | Ninth year of summer, 298 AC | About 08:00
Elapsed: None; opening situation.

The holdfast's men had cleared a place beside the chopping block. One carried away a basket of split billets. Another drove a wedge beneath the short leg of the bench and tested it with his weight.

The prisoner sat against the wall, still bound. His black cloak had been taken from him. A guard folded it, found something in the lining and put it aside without showing it to the others.

“I can stand,” the prisoner said.

The guards had to lift him. His knees folded before they had him clear of the wall. They kept him between them while Jory checked the cord at his wrists, loosened one turn and tied it again.

Jon's garron was with the other horses. Bran stood close enough to ask him something without calling across the yard. Robb was on Bran's other side; Theon had gone nearer the block.

Lord Eddard waited until the guards had brought the man to him.

“Were you ordered to leave?”

“No.”

“Where are the men you went out with?”

“There were three of us.”

“You've said that.”

The prisoner looked past him toward the gate. “I left them.”

One of the holdfast men stopped shifting the billets. Jory told him to finish clearing the place, and he took up the basket again. The prisoner asked for water. A guard brought it in a wooden cup and held it for him while he drank.

Bran watched their father. He had been told to come and watch, and no one had given him anything else to do.

“Was leaving all he did?” he asked Jon.

Eddard had taken Ice from its scabbard. The guards were bringing the prisoner to the block, slowly because he could not stand unaided. Jory moved the empty scabbard out of their way. Bran waited beside Jon.
