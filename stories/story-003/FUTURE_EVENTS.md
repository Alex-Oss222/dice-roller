## How the clock operates

Follow [TURN_PROCESS.md](../../rules/TURN_PROCESS.md). Keep codes, countdowns, status labels and dependencies out of narrative, headers and the player-facing changes. Show actual events, observations, messages and recollections instead. An explicit OOC countdown request can receive a separate answer with ordinary event names.

- Advance only through time authorized by action, travel, work or waiting. Reading, setup maintenance, a new chat or real time passing advances nothing.
- Before resolving an interval, identify due windows, working dates, projects, injuries and reports in transit. Process chronologically; the original outline's thematic order is not necessarily event order.
- For events without an exact day, preserve that historical uncertainty and assign a plausible campaign working date or narrower window as play approaches. Do not postpone eligible events forever because the books give no day. Label authored dates as such.
- Recheck prerequisites whenever circumstances change, including acceleration before a historical date. Intentions and plans alone do not establish successful prevention. Accomplished preparation in the live state matters without requiring the player to rebuild it.
- Adjudicate attempts and results separately. A mission can occur while its victim, route, survival or losses change. Historical counts and deaths are references, not automatic deductions or immunity.
- Keep occurrence, report departure, report arrival and personal learning distinct. A world fact is not automatically in Jon's knowledge. Recollection of a historical event is not confirmation that it has just occurred in this run.
- Pause at a consequential choice belonging to Jon. The calendar never authorizes his pledge, military expedition, judgment, marriage, disclosure or royal bargain. It likewise does not return ordinary decisions explicitly delegated to Arra for needless approval.
- Do not force later history back to its original course. Changed allegiances, deaths, survival, routes, outbreaks and actual northern dispatches alter descendants. Unrelated causes may remain intact.
- Period summaries and their component events overlap. Apply a death, payment, loss, capture or succession once, not again when reading a summary. A defeated army is not automatically a counted number of dead men.
