## Transfer, memory and competence

In 124 Bennard and his eldest son Benjen arranged saddle sabotage through a bribed stablehand. Cregan's fall caused a severe head injury: his consciousness died permanently and his body survived. The Old Gods drew Jon through the weirwoods from 487 into that body. Jon's former body died; no weapons, animals or other possessions accompanied him. This was a singular, nonrepeatable transfer, not a new ability or a rescue quest for Cregan.

Jon retains his own 204 years of memory and Cregan's memories in full, including childhood, family, friendships, education and knowledge of Winterfell. Jon's own personality and accumulated experience remain his. Full inherited memory supplies neither unknown conspiracies nor another person's thoughts. He uncovered the assassination through investigation and evidence during 125, not automatic transfer knowledge.

Recovery occupied the remainder of 124. By 125 he was entirely comfortable with Cregan's movement, strength, swordsmanship and limits. Do not restore first-day adjustment, recognition problems, clumsiness, neurological impairment or deficits from discarded runs. Old-body scars, aging and a slight speed advantage in the 487 profile are not automatically properties of Cregan's body. No measurement proves either body superior. Jon remains mortal and subject to ordinary injury, fatigue, healing and situational limits.

He retains the judgment and learning of an experienced commander, a king who ruled for sixty-two years, and a scholar and traveller with over a century of post-abdication experience. His specialisms, languages, warging, glass-candle experience, autobiographical memory and their limits are preserved in the source profile. No glass candle, Longclaw, Lysene spear, Ghost, Huginn, Muninn or former manor is present merely because that earlier record names it. His memory is not an infallible photographic library; skill does not grant current intelligence or mastery of every craft.

**Equipment established in Turn 16:** Ice, the existing Stark Valyrian greatsword, travelled in Jon's campaign baggage and was brought out at his direction. It is a local house possession, not an imported 487 weapon, purchase or newly forged sword. Light whetstone passes over its keen edge produced no new power, damage or special improvement. The grove called a godswood by the camp is authored local staging beside the ruins; no intact canonical enclosure or carved heart tree was established.
