## Turn 18 | 129/04/05 | Late evening
Elapsed: Forty-five minutes.

Jacaerys listened through the revised offer, then asked the clerk to strike out the six-moon campaign and the proposed remission of Winterfell's levy.

“Two years at most, from the host's passage out of the Neck,” he said. “Less if the war ends sooner. Your commander, your consent to detachments, and your choice of campaigns in my mother's service. Daemon will receive that article with the others. I will not describe you to him as a captain placed under his orders.”

Jon's undertaking was entered beside it: northern service to secure Rhaenyra's victory, with assistance to Daemon when Jon judged it useful to that cause. Neither the number twelve thousand nor an immediate departure was added to the article.

“For the ports and trading fleet, yes. Bear Island under Mormont, White Harbor with Manderly. The right to build, repair, provision and trade will be explicit, including voyages to Braavos. The willing shipwrights and officers we discussed can be engaged under the same grant.”

The clerk read back the customs clause. Jacaerys stopped him at the first rate.

“Nine dragons where the ordinary duty would be ten, for two years. Then eleven where it would be ten, for four. After that, the ordinary duty again. Write all six years. The later payments do not disappear because the war is over.”

He accepted the schedule in place of the earlier levy concession. It applied to the Crown's customs on qualifying northern imports and exports; separate agreements would govern the local charges belonging to Manderly and Corlys. Braavos would still set its own dues.

“The works must be named, and their completion witnessed,” the prince added. “Not a date chosen by my collectors, nor a harbour declared forever unfinished because another warehouse is wanted.”

The clerk left the construction schedule for the surveys and agreed plans. No completed port, fleet or royal building subsidy was entered as something already delivered.

A servant brought a jug from the camp. “Two cups, my lord.” There was one on the tray. He set it on the fallen wall and went back for the other.

Jacaerys returned to the city article.

“I will back both charters, with the powers you have named and the landholders' consent. I will also speak for the reciprocal trading agreement at Dragonstone and put the Driftmark terms before my grandfather myself. I am promising my support in those negotiations, not pretending his seal is mine.”

He did not pass over the Gift.

“You now ask for my mother's writ and her support in a settlement with the Watch. I will recommend the restoration of both grants to Stark, with the six tenths for the brothers preserved. I will argue for it before her. But the writ must come from her, and the Watch will still have to be dealt with. My agreement tonight does not put their land in your possession.”

He had the clerk retain the restoration article rather than reduce it to a request for an inquiry. The proposed division remained six tenths to the Watch and four to Winterfell; its accounting terms still needed to be defined. Nothing in the new papers revoked an existing agreement with the brothers.

The clerk made two fair copies, numbered the leaves and threaded them together through holes along the upper edge. He read each clause against the draft. Jacaerys checked the command article once more, then signed his endorsement and pressed his seal into the wax beneath it.

“On these articles, you have my agreement to take them to the queen and my word that I will urge her to accept them as a whole. I will not go home asking for your army on different terms.”

He left one endorsed copy with Jon. The royal writs, customs grant and confirmation of the military articles still required Rhaenyra's answer; no oath or marching order was taken in their place. Jacaerys asked that his riding gear be ready before dawn. “I shall leave in the morning. When I return, I mean to bring you her answer under seal.”

### Changed this turn

Jacaerys has endorsed Jon's revised articles for royal ratification: independent northern command and campaigning in Rhaenyra's cause for up to two years, port and merchant-fleet permissions, the proposed customs schedule, support for two city charters and separate trade agreements, and advocacy for a royal writ restoring both Gifts. This replaces the six-moon service proposal and the earlier levy-relief proposals.

The customs proposal is 90% of standard royal duties for two years after certified completion of the agreed works, then 110% for four years, then standard duties. It is not an exemption currently in force or a grant of another lord's or Braavos's customs.

Jacaerys intends to leave for Dragonstone at dawn with the endorsed articles. Rhaenyra has not ratified them; no land has changed hands and no northern march has begun. Arra's signed Watch settlement and implementation authority remain intact.
