## Turn 0 | Third day of the first moon, 126 AC | Morning

Elapsed: None; opening situation after the established backstory.

The steward brought the household returns to the solar. Jon had the previous account open beside him, with his own corrections in the margin.

“Only the household papers this morning.”

A sealed letter from White Harbor lay beneath them.

The steward untied the linen tape, separated the returns by estate and placed their covering sheets beside the ledger. The clerk trimmed his quill, poured a little ink into the horn and laid a clean sheet on the writing board. A second bundle held the latest reports on provisions and the roads under repair.

Jon knew the men and their work. He had chosen which duties to leave with the steward and which reports were to come directly to him. The older entries recorded orders already given; the fresh papers concerned their execution.

“The harbor letter came with the porter,” the steward said. “I haven't opened it.”

He put it beside the writing board and waited. The clerk left room at the head of the sheet for Jon's instructions.
