## Arra: disclosure, answer and early practice

After the midday meal on the third, Jon privately told Arra his name, his remembered life through 487 AC and his arrival in Cregan's body that morning. He said that he lacks Cregan's private memories and does not know Cregan's fate or whether he will return. He disclaimed any right to her friendship or possible love for Cregan and asked for discretion and help understanding the present.

Arra answered after the midday meal on the sixth in Turn 4. She offers discreet practical help with identifying people, household and mountain-family customs, recent events she witnessed, and identifying people better informed than herself. She has kept Jon's account private and says she will not make it household talk. She remains uncertain of the transfer and Cregan's disappearance, and will not attest Jon's account as proven fact. This is practical cooperation, not a formal office, unconditional lifetime oath or promise to support every future action.

She suggests Jon consider a visit to the maester and offers to accompany him, without making it a condition of helping. Jon has neither accepted nor refused that suggestion, and no appointment, examination or wider disclosure has occurred. The three-day consideration period is completed, not extended or forgotten.

The conversation occurred in a checked shallow recess screened from the path by an outcrop near the inner wall of the godswood, with guards left outside. No one overheard it and no wider disclosure has occurred by the end of Turn 4. This successful private conversation does not establish a soundproof site, exclusive Stark knowledge or guaranteed privacy in later visits.

At Jon's request, Arra approached Harl, the better swordsman of her two accompanying Norrey men. Harl came that afternoon and personally agreed to practice at first light in Winterfell's practice yard on the fourth, fifth and sixth, ending sooner if Arra answers. He arranged escort coverage with his companion. Blunted swords and padded coats were used. All three sessions on the fourth, fifth and sixth are now complete. Jon consistently created controlled touches through timing, changes of line and blade control against Harl's competent resistance. The sixth retained the earlier adjustments and ended without injury. The temporary arrangement has ended; no further practice is scheduled. Neither man was injured. He knows only that the lord requested practice. This is temporary cooperation, not a transfer of service, exceptional combat mastery or a new paid appointment.

## Bennard: proposals through Turn 4

On the third, Bennard requested supervised rooms, meals and exercise for his three sons at Winterfell. He offered continued confinement for himself, public acknowledgment of Cregan's sole authority and an instruction to his sons to obey. His assertion that they merely followed his orders remains his claim.

Jon countered with survival for Bennard and his sons in exchange for knowledge and records of northern dealings. The sons would have livelihoods but no keep or office of rule or importance. He also proposed Watch service for male descendants, selected marriages for female descendants and an unsupported winter hunt for Bennard. The hunt's duration, equipment, food, shelter, permitted trade, whether sons accompanied him, and scope of any ban on aid were never specified. Do not turn this into exposure, exile or a scheduled death by inference. Jon's claim that Bennard would not have shown mercy is an accusation, not known counterfactual fact.

On the sixth Bennard rejected the undefined hunt and restrictions on unborn descendants. He offered an account of his regency, letters, promises and dealings, checked against records, continued self-confinement and acknowledgment of Cregan, while urging his sons to accept no keep, command or governing office. He sought their supervised maintenance at Winterfell until a definite lawful livelihood and residence were agreed. His sons had not assented. Jon heard this without accepting a complete settlement; no testimony had yet been supplied under the bargain. Turn 5's current orders and limited cooperation supersede that custody position below.
