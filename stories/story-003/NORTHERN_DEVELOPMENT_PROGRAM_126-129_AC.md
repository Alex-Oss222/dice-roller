**Story:** 003. **Status:** planning reference only, not enacted. **Prepared against the Story 003 state at the sixth day of the fourth moon, 126 AC, afternoon.** **Final audit deadline:** end of the first moon, 129 AC. **Accounting moon:** 30 days. **Units:** gold dragons (GD), 210 silver stags/GD, 8 bushels/quarter.

> **This is one integrated programme, superseding the earlier illustrative 50,000 GD allocation. It is not 50,000 GD plus every new proposal added again.** It incorporates the earlier regional road, bridge, well, storage and wharf portfolio; assigns overlapping works to the named houses exactly once; adds the new castle, Winter Town, rural-estate, bathhouse and industrial proposals; and explicitly includes all 35 direct bannermen and 16 subordinate houses. No spreadsheets are to be created or added to the repository for this programme. Keep the accounts as tables and records in this Markdown document or other explicitly authorized Markdown files only.

The opening treasury in Story 003 is 250,000 GD; 70,777 quarters of recorded Stark grain are spread across the lordship, not necessarily inside Winterfell. ### Public baths: seven distinct locations, not seven guaranteed profits

### Tannery and rural hide-house connection

The Winter Town tanner must have an industrial site **below drinking-water sources** and a controlled discharge. Vegetable hide tanning commonly occupies hides in processing for **roughly a year or longer**, so a new shed finished after 6–12 moons does **not** guarantee cured leather 15 moons after programme authorization. Plan initial finished batches **approximately 18–30 moons after starting**, subject to available pits and the date hides are first put into cure. Fifty vats must be tested for realistic hide capacity and bark supply.

The demesne produces **23,876 slaughter cattle annually** in the fiscal model; its 20% modeled lord's share would correspond to at most **about 4,775 associated hides**, *if the hides are separately controlled and not already included in a sale of whole cattle*. Raw hide **0.159042 GD/hide** and tanned hide **0.397606 GD/hide** are book catalog prices.

If the venture earns **half the price differential** on 4,775 actual suitable hides, the new gross margin is **4,775 × (0.397606 − 0.159042) ÷ 2 = 569.57 GD/year**. At a speculative **90 GD/year** combined operating allowance (INCLUDING the **62 GD/year** proposed for the rural hide house, rather than subtracting that 62 twice), remaining margin is **479.57 GD before an incremental Crown charge**, or approximately **407.64 GD** if a 15% net remittance charge applies. That is a favorable scenario, **not a guaranteed return**: fifty vats, bark, salting, waste handling, labor and long inventory residence may cost more than 90 GD. Require a detailed bark, vat-capacity, hides-in-process and wage account before using the forecast.

**Winter Town approval gates:** confirm market privileges; signed warehouse lessees; measured demand for baths; wool pilot and buyer terms; suitable fulling stream and machine design; tannery effluent route, bark and pit capacity. Halt or reduce works that do not pass.

### 4E. Commercial claims that require corrected arithmetic or extra evidence

**Market and fair:** 1% applies only to taxable transactions, not all gross commerce, and **0.0002 GD per stall-day** is a negligible fee. At 50,000 GD taxable value and 100 paid stalls over 14 days, gross fees are **500.28 GD**, not 500 GD net. Deduct the **75 GD** cost, leaving **425.28 GD before whatever tribute, tax or profit-sharing actually applies**. Winter Town's illustrative incremental 15% Crown charge would leave **361.49 GD**. Do not copy this Crown rate mechanically to a bannerman's separate revenue.

**Cloth:** a 1,005.99 GD physical plant may not produce the same result as Winter Town. Six master weavers per site from Manderly's factors is a **request for eighteen masters among three ventures**, not proof that eighteen are available. If local lordships fail to find trained labor or mills by 127, revert to lower-volume spinning, weaving and foot-fulling trials instead of booking 454 GD/year automatically.

**Fish:** 10 stations at 331 GD or 20 stations at 663 GD produce no automatic 912/1,824 GD local income. Cost raw catch, salt, drying-weight loss, actual seasonal output, port dues and merchant receipts by house. Keep separate northern and eastern catch accounts. “First call on one-tenth dried fish” is a **right of first purchase at market price**, not an uncompensated levy.

**Tar:** the estimated 132-gallon burn and four burns/year depend on resin-rich timber, adequate woodland, fuel, careful tending and buyers. Avoid multiplying identical prices for **Stark's 20 rural kilns and Hornwood's separate 20** without testing demand. Timber already charged a wood-lease royalty is not royalty-charged again at the same stage without explicit contractual substitution.

**Tanning:** bark-tanning can occupy hides for roughly 12–18 moons once processing begins. Dustin's tannery and Winter Town's tannery require **different hides**, workers, pits and lease rights. A warehouse price is not by itself the price of complete fifty-vat leather production.
