## Normal play

Tell the GM what your character tries to do.

Example:

```text
Continue story-002.

My choice: I ask my father privately whether the deserter truly believed what he said.
```

The GM should:

1. Load the current character and relevant world state.
2. Adjudicate the action from established facts, abilities, relationships, opposition, and circumstances.
3. Write the narrative.
4. Save only lasting changes that could matter later.
5. Stop when the player has another meaningful decision.

The player does not maintain JSON, mechanical ratings, world records, or generated pages by hand.

## Fresh chat

Use:

```text
Use Alex-Oss222/dice-roller. Continue story-00N from its saved state. My next decision is: [action].
```

The repository is the durable record. Chat memory is not required.
