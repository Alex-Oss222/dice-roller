# A Song of Blood & Gold: character sheet

**Status: awaiting setup. This is a blank template, not an initialized character or Turn 1.**

This is a shared blank template. Each new story requires its own character document at that story's root `character-sheet.md`, read first and completed before initialization. After setup it preserves starting input; current state comes from accepted events and that story's generated `play/character-sheet.md`. See [the isolated story layout](../stories/README.md) for journals, current sheets, resume views, and saves.

The tables below support the optional [Blood & Gold capability module](../rules/capabilities.md). Select it for this particular story rather than inheriting another story's choice. Common [play rules](../rules/iron_engine.md), [book references](../references/books.md), and distance data stay shared; NPCs, claims, outcomes, knowledge, assumptions, and special agreements stay local.

`TBD` means a setup fact is still to be supplied. `Not established` means no supported finding has been recorded. Neither means zero, healthy, absent, owned, or available. Use `none` only after absence is actually established. Empty example rows create no people, property, wounds, obligations, or skills.

### Experience

Write four to six paragraphs describing how the character came to their strongest abilities and where those abilities stop. Each paragraph covers one area of life (arms, travel, household, letters, faith, trade, whatever the character actually has) and names the practice, instruction, service or hardship behind the ratings recorded below, in the voice of a biography rather than a rules note. Give the low ratings the same treatment: a plain sentence on what the character never learned or never had reason to. Do not write "in this campaign" or "for this version of the character"; the notes folder holds the distinction between sourced history and the player's premise. Do not include instructions to the GM, disclaimers about evidence, or lists of things that are not established.
